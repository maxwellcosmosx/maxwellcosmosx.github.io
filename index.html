About Me

I am a Chinese born and raised in Yanshi. When I was a boy, I was nobody's idea of a promising child—I cultivated no real interests, pursued no hobbies, and spent most of my time in front of a screen playing video games. That was essentially the whole of it. The one exception was mathematics. I was always decent at it, almost in spite of myself, and over time I came to actually like it. Not for any grand reason, but because it made me feel I was smarter than others thought.

I got a BSc in Physics at Nankai University. I also was enchanted with computers and played around with my laptop — not learning institutionalized computer science, but tinkering with things I enjoyed on it, such as trying out Linux and configuring interesting programs like vim, zsh, st, and so on. At the same time, I studied English, Japanese and German, and I left China to further improve languages. I am still finding what I like as a career.

The most comforting verse: But thou, O LORD, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head.

Ecclesiastes 1:3-11

What do people gain from all their labors
at which they toil under the sun?
Generations come and generations go,
but the earth remains forever.
The sun rises and the sun sets,
and hurries back to where it rises.
The wind blows to the south
and turns to the north;
round and round it goes,
ever returning on its course.
All streams flow into the sea,
yet the sea is never full.
To the place the streams come from,
there they return again.
All things are wearisome,
more than one can say.
The eye never has enough of seeing,
nor the ear its fill of hearing.
What has been will be again,
what has been done will be done again;
there is nothing new under the sun.
Is there anything of which one can say,
"Look! This is something new"?
It was here already, long ago;
it was here before our time.
No one remembers the former generations,
and even those yet to come
will not be remembered
by those who follow them.
