***情态动词***

情态动词汇总与整理有两个难点:
1. 时态。有些情况下, 过去式就是纯粹的时态变形, 含义完全没变, 需要放在一起。 但是在有些情况下, 表面虽是过去式, 实际语义已经独立分化, 必须单独完整保留。
2. 散落。拥有同一功能但表示程度不同的表达散落在各个情态动词板块里, 需要摘出来并按照程度的变化整理成列表。
这样处理后, 每个情态动词自己的板块里, 只保留它独有的、 进不了上述任何一张对比表的用法。

另外需要说明的两点:
1. 虚拟语气及转述都会牵涉情态动词的使用, 需另开文章说明。
2. 有一些独立的固定搭配也用到了情态动词, 在这里不作收录。
------------------------------------------------------------

Table I. 让他人去做
1. Will you vp.?		你能..吗?		#礼貌程度(I)#
   Will you pass me the salt?
   
2. Can you vp.?		你能..吗?		#礼貌程度(II)#
   Can you pass me the salt?

3. Would you vp.?		你能..吗?		#礼貌程度(III)#
   Would you pass me the salt?

4. Could you vp.?		你能..吗?		#礼貌程度(III)#
   Could you pass me the salt?

5. Would you mind vp_ger.?		你介意..吗?		#礼貌程度(MAX)#
   Would you mind passing me the salt?
-------------------------------------------------------------

Table II. 请求他人的允许
1. Can I vp.?		我能..吗?		#礼貌程度(II)#
   Can I leave early today?

2. Could I vp.		我能..吗?		#礼貌程度(III)#
   Could I ask a quick question?

3. May I vp.?		我能..吗?		#礼貌程度(MAX)#
   May I ask you something?

4. Might I vp.?		我能..吗?		#礼貌程度(MAX) 邮件用语#
   Might I suggest an alternative?
--------------------------------------------------------------

Table III. 给他人建议
1. You had better vp.	你最好给我..		#礼貌程度(I)#
   You had better eat it all.

2. You should vp.		你应该..		#礼貌程度(II)#
   You should apologize.

3. You could vp.		你可以..		#礼貌程度(III)#
   You could try calling customer service.
   
4. You might wanna vp.		你或许需要..		#礼貌程度(MAX-)#
   You might want to back up your files before the update. 

5. I would suggest vp_ger.		我建议..		#礼貌程度(MAX)#	
   I would suggest waiting a bit.

7. must vp.		肯定是..了		#确定程度(MAX)#

**shall**
1. shall vp.		必须..		#用在正式规定之中,比must更正式#
   The tenant shall pay the rent on the first day of each month. 

2. Shall I vp.?		我要不要..?		#主动提议#
   Shall I open the window?
   
3. Shall we vp.?		我们..如何?		#邀请#
   Shall we begin the meeting?

4. I/We shall vp.		我(们)必将..		#庄严#
   We shall never surrender. 
   I shall return.
------------------------------------------------------------

**should**
1. should vp.		最好要..		#应该#
   People should be kind to each other.

2. Why should I vp.?		我凭什么..?!		#反问#
   Why should I apologize? I didn't do anything wrong.

3. I should think/say (that) ...		我觉得..		#表达自我认为,谦逊有修养#
   I should say it'll be fine.

4. should've vp_pp		本应该..		#事实上并没有做,后悔#
   I should've left earlier, now we're stuck in traffic.

5. shouldn't have vp_pp		本不应该..		#事实上已经做了,后悔#
   I shouldn't have eaten so much.
   
6. Should you vp.		如果你..		#商务书信重要语法#
   Should you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
   
7. 固定搭配 
   How should I know?!
   You should have seen it!
   You should have been there!
-------------------------------------------------------------

**will**
1. will vp.		将会..		#并没有提前好好计划#	...(1)
   I will call her later.

2. I'll vp.		我去..		#当即状态下决定去做#
   The phone is ringing, I'll answer it.

3. I/We will vp.		我(们)定会..		#承诺#
   We will finish the project on time.
   I will never leave you. 

4. That'll be sth.		我猜那是..		#即时猜测#
   That'll be the postman.

5. will often vp.		总是..		#习惯#
   She will often stay up late working.
   
6. will vp.		老是..		#抱怨他人的坏习惯#
   He will interrupt people mid-sentence, it drives me crazy.
   
7. won't vp.	将不会..		#will的简单否定#
   I won't be home tonight.

8. won't vp.		就是不..		#顽固不配合,超级地道#
   No matter what I do, the car won't start.

9. Won't you vp.?		你不..吗?		#地道的邀请#
   Won't you come in?
   
10. 固定搭配
    Will you marry me?

(1) be going to vp.	将会..		#提前好好计划#
    I'm going to visit my parents this weekend.
-----------------------------------------------------------

**would**
1. Would you like sth.?		要来点..吗?		#Do you want sth.的礼貌表达#
   Would you like some coffee?

2. I would say (that) ...		我觉得..		#表达自我认为,I think的口语版#
   I would say it's a good idea.
   
3. I wouldn't say (that) ...		我不觉得..		#表达自我认为,I don't think的口语版#
   I wouldn't say he's the best candidate. 
	
4. I would like/love/prefer to vp.		我想要去..		#I want to vp.的礼貌表达#
   I would like to order now. 

5. would rather vp.		我更愿意..		#比较后给出自己愿意的答案#
   I would rather stay home tonight. 

6. would vp.		过去常常..		#过去常做,只能跟动作#	...(1)
   When I was a kid, my grandfather would tell me stories before bed.

7. What would you do if ...		如果..你会怎么做?		#固定搭配#
   What would you do if you were me?

8. Would you believe (that) ...?		你敢信..?		#固定搭配#
   Would you believe he forgot my name?

9. wouldn't vp.		就是不..		#顽固不配合,超级地道 won't的过去式#
   The car wouldn't start this morning.
	
10. would've vp_pp.		本会..的		#事实上没做#
    I would've said something stupid.

11. wouldn't have vp_pp.		本不会..的		#事实上做了#
    I wouldn't have failed the exam.

12. would vp.		老是..		#抱怨他人的坏习惯#
    He would always show up late and never apologize.

(1) used to vp.		过去常常..	#过去常做,可以是动作或状态#
    I used to live in London.
-------------------------------------------------------------

**can**
1. can vp.		能..		#能力#
   I can speak three languages.

2. can vp.		有可能..		#可能性#
   It can get very cold here in winter.
   Smoking can cause cancer.

3. can vp.		可以..		#表示许可#
   You can use my car.

4. I can + smell/see/hear		我闻到/看到/听到..了		#代替进行时,十分地道#
   I can hear someone knocking at the door.

5. I can't believe (that) ...		我不敢相信..		#表示惊讶,固定搭配#
   I can't believe I passed the exam.

6. Can I have sth.?		我要..		#点餐买东西#
   Can I have a coffee?
	
7. Can't you just vp.?		你就不能..吗?		#极度不耐烦#
   Can't you just be quiet for a minute?
---------------------------------------------------------------

**could**
1. could vp.		能..		#能力,can的过去式#
   I couldn't understand him at all.

2. How could you vp.		你怎么能..		#震惊责备#
   How could you say that to her!

3. I could vp.		我简直能..		#夸张表示强烈情绪#
   I could kill him right now!
   I could scream!

4. could've vp_pp.		本能够..		#事实上没能#
   You could've told me earlier.

5. couldn't have vp_pp.		本不能够..		#事实上确实做到了#
   I couldn't have won on my own, but he was late and forfeited.

6. 固定搭配
   I couldn't agree more.
   Things couldn't be better/worse.
   I couldn't care less.
-----------------------------------------------------------

**may**
1. may vp.		能..		#表示允许,正式书面#
   You may begin the exam now.

2. may vp.,but ...		可能..但是..		#表示让步,后跟转折#
   It may be expensive, but it's worth every penny.

3. 固定搭配
   May you have a wonderful year ahead.
   May God bless you.
-----------------------------------------------------------

**might**
1. might have vp_pp.	本应该..		#对他人的委婉责备#
   You might have told me!
   
2. might as well vp.		干脆就..吧		#干脆#
   Since you're here, you might as well stay for dinner.
------------------------------------------------------------

**must**
1. mustn't vp.		不可以..		#表示禁止#
   You mustn't smoke here. 

2. must vp.		必须..			#命令他人必须照做,基本不用于疑问句#	...(1)
   You must fasten your seatbelt while driving.

3. You must vp.	强烈推荐你..		#强烈推荐#
   You must visit that museum—it's amazing! 

4. I must vp. 		我必须得..		#个人决心#
   I must finish this report tonight.

5. Must you vp.?		你非得..吗?		#极度不耐烦#
   Must you interrupt every time I'm talking?

6. 固定搭配
   You must be joking
   This must be a mistake
   
(1) have (got) to vp.		必须..		#客观不得不,地道口语,可以用于疑问句#
    I have got to submit this assignment by Friday.
