
***方位描述***

三种用法:
1. be动词
2. 副词
3. There be
-------------------------------------------------------

**空间方位**
1. be above sth.		在..上方		#不接触#
   The clock is above the fireplace.
   The painting is hanging above the sofa.
   There is a fan above the dining table.

2. be below sth.		在..下方		#不接触#
   The basement is below the ground floor.
   The river flows below the bridge.
   There is a parking lot below the building.
   
3. be on top of sth.		在...上面		#接触#	
   My keys are on top of the dresser.
   A cat is sleeping on top of my car.
   There is a cat on top of the car.

4. be underneath sth.		在..下面		#接触#
   His passport was underneath a pile of papers.
   The roach ran underneath the sofa.
   There is a soft layer underneath the carpet.

5. be under sth.		在...下面		#万能#
   The shoes are under the bed.
   A dog is resting under the tree.
   There is a dog under the tree.

6. be at the top of sth.		在..上部		#自身部分#
   The eagle's nest is at the top of the tree.
   I'll wait for you at the top of the mountain.
   There is a lighthouse at the top of the cliff.

7. be at the bottom of sth.		在..下部		#自身部分#
   The treasure was at the bottom of the lake.
   Press the button at the bottom of the dashboard.
   There is some sediment at the bottom of the bottle.
-------------------------------------------------------
1. be in front of sth.		在..前方		#在外部#
   The car is in front of the house.
   A bus parked in front of the store.
   There is a big tree in front of the building.

2. be behind sth.		在..后方		#在外部#
   The garden is behind the house.
   The sun disappeared behind the clouds.
   There is a small alley behind the restaurant.

3. be in the front of sth.		在..前部		#在内部#
   The driver is in the front of the bus.
   She sat in the front of the classroom.
   There are two speakers in the front of the car.

4. be in the back of sth.		在..后部		#在内部#
   The luggage is in the back of the trunk.
   The kids fell asleep in the back of the van.
   There is a spare tire in the back of the truck.

5. be at the back of sth.		在..最后端		#自身部分#
   The table of contents is at the front of the book.
   He stood at the front of the line.
   There is a sharp point at the front of the ship.

6. be at the front of sth.		在..最前端		#自身部分#
   The answers are at the back of the textbook.
   She walked at the back of the parade.
   There is a fire exit at the back of the hallway.

4. be to the south of sth.		在..南方

   There is a soccer field to the south of the library.

**时间方位**
be sn. minutes away from sth.		距..几分钟的路程
My office is just 5 minutes away from my apartment.

be a sn.-minute drive/ride/walk from sth.		距..走路/骑车/开车几分钟的路程
The mall is a 15-minute drive from my house.
It's about a 20-minute bike ride from downtown.


be a sn.-minute drive east/west/north/south of sth.		在..东/西/北/南几分钟的路程
