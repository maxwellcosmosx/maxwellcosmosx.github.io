**方式**
1. walk + on sth.		在..上走
   Please don't walk on the freshly painted floor.
   
2. walk + across sth.		穿过..		#表面穿过#
   Be careful when you walk across the busy intersection.
   
3. walk + through sth.		穿过..		#内部穿过#
   We walked through the old town, admiring the narrow alleys.

4. walk + towards sth.		向着..走
   He walked towards the stage as his name was called.
 
5. walk + away from sth.		背离..走
   He walked away from the burning building as soon as possible.
 
6. walk + into wth.		走进..
   She walked into the bakery on the corner and bought a croissant.

7. walk + out of sth.		走出..
   I walked out of the subway station and immediately felt the cold wind.   

8. walk + around sth.		在..区域内走		#大区域#
   We spent the whole afternoon walking around the city center.
   
9. walk + around sth.		绕着..走		#小建筑#
   The cat walked around the box, sniffing it curiously.
   
10. walk + past sth.		走经..
    I walked past my old school on the way home and felt nostalgic.

11. walk + by sth.		走经..
    Did you see the mural when you walked by the park?

12. walk + up sth.		沿着..走		#在上面走#
    Walk up Fifth Avenue and you'll see the museum on your left.

13. walk + down sth.		沿着..走		#在上面走#
    Walk down this street and you'll see the museum on your right.

14. walk + down by sth.		沿着..走		#在旁边走#
    We walked down by the river, enjoying the view.

15. walk + along sth.		沿着..走上	#宽泛,可指在旁边走、也可指在上面走#
    They walked along the beach, watching the sunset.
---------------------------------------------------------------------

**方向**
1. walk + up north
   I'm heading up north for the weekend.

2. walk + down south
   They usually go down south to Florida during the winter.

3. walk + out west
   My sister moved out west to California last year.

4. walk + back east
   Are you going back east to see your parents over Thanksgiving?
