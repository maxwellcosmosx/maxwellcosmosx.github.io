***使役动词***

-----------------------------------------------
get so. + to vp.		说服..做..		#说服、劝诱#
I'll try to get him to help us with the project.

get so. + vp_prp.
I got the kids playing outside.

get sth. + vp_pp.		
I wanna get my hair dyed.
-----------------------------------------------
have so. + vp.		安排..做..		#安排、指派#
I'll have my assistant call you.

have so. + vp_prp.
I had him waiting outside for an hour.

have sth. + vp_pp.
We're having the house painted next week.
-----------------------------------------------
let so. + vp.	让..做..		#允许、放任#
Don't disturb him, let him cook.
-----------------------------------------------
make so. + vp.		迫使..做..		#逼迫、迫使#
The situation made her rethink her decision.
-----------------------------------------------
