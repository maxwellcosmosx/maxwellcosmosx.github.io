***连系动词(Linking Verbs)***

连系动词(Linking Verbs)后面可以直接加形容词,表示一种状态,大致分为四类:
1. 感受类
2. 变化类
3. 保持类
4. 结果类
-------------------------------------------------
1. be		是..的		#处于某种状态#
   The weather is cold today.
-------------------------------------------------

**感受类**
1. taste		尝起来..		
   This coffee tastes bitter.

2. smell		闻起来..		
   The flowers smell sweet.

3. feel		感觉起来..		
   I feel excited about the trip.

4. look		看起来..		
   This dress looks beautiful.

5. sound		听起来..		
   That sounds interesting.

6. appear		看起来似乎..
   She appeared nervous during the interview.

7. Seem		看起来似乎..
   The food seems delicious.
-----------------------------------------------------

**变化类**
1. get		变得..		#口语、随意#
   It's getting dark.

2. go		变..		#负面、糟糕#
   The milk went bad.

3. become		变得..		#正式、万能#
   He became angry after hearing the news.

4. grow		变得..		#中性#
   His hair grew longer during the summer.

5. turn		变..
   His face turned pale when he heard the news.
   
6. run		变..
   We are running low on gas.
   The river ran dry last summer.
   
7. fall		变..
   He fell ill during the trip.
   The room suddenly fell silent.
----------------------------------------------------

**保持类**
1. keep		保持..
   I was struggling to keep awake.

2. stay		保持..
   Please stay quiet during the meeting.

3. remain		保持..
   The situation remained tense after the meeting.

4. hold		保持..
   The rule still holds true today.
   This offer will hold good until next Monday.
---------------------------------------------------

**结果类**
1. prove		证明是..
   The plan proved successful.

2. end up		最终变得..
   The conversation ended up awkward.

3. turn out		结果是..
   Everything turned out fine.
---------------------------------------------------
