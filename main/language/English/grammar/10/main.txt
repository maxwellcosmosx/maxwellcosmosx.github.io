***插入语***

Filler Words在口语中至关重要, 如果不懂插入语,很可能会影响到理解对话,插入语一般起到:
1. 话语标记
2. 占位思考
3. 缓冲语气
4. 强化情绪
5. 增加节奏
-------------------------------------------------------

**话语标记**
1. Here's the thing

2. Look
   Look, I'm not trying to argue with you.
   
3. Listen

4. Wait

5. I mean

6. Let's say

7. Anyway
-------------------------------------------------------

**占位思考**
1. like

2. well

3. you know
-------------------------------------------------------

**缓冲语气**
1. or something

2. sorta (sort of)

3. kinda (kind of)

4. I guess
-------------------------------------------------------

**强化情绪**
1. literally

2. as fuck
I'm tired as fuck today!

3. to death
She scared me to death!

4. the shit out of

5. the hell out of

6. the hell

7. the heck

8. the fuck

9. the freak

10. in the world

11. on earth
----------------------------------------------------
